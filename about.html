Geiser is an experimental duo from southern Sweden, formed by Christian Nanzell and David Lomelino, both musicians and producers with a long history in various bands, now united in a project where the organic takes center stage.

Their sound moves between the cinematic and the electronic. Synth textures blend with natural rhythms, field recordings, typewriter clicks, and small tactile details that make the music feel alive. It’s minimal yet exploratory, simple yet full of intention. With Geiser, the duo aims to create music that isn’t just heard, but felt.
